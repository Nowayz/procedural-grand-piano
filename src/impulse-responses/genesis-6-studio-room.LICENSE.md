# Genesis 6 studio-room impulse response

This impulse response comes from the **Open Acoustic Impulse Response Library
(OpenAIR)** of the AudioLab, University of York.

- Space: Genesis 6 Studio live recording room, University of York
- Measurement team: Daniel Johnston and Panagiotis Kompotis
- Capture date: 17 February 2017
- Room volume: 35.2 m³, acoustically treated
- Capture: Soundfield ST350, 96 kHz/24-bit, logarithmic swept sine
- Dataset: https://webfiles.york.ac.uk/OPENAIR/IRs/genesis-6-studio-live-room-drum-set/
- Source archive SHA-256:
  `f4d47d33628a27cb4eb343a30581952482aa6f1853392bdb909e6137b8680b64`
- Attribution: www.openairlib.net; Genesis 6, University of York
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The eight-source average B-format response was decoded to front-facing stereo
using `L = 0.707W + 0.5X + 0.5Y` and `R = 0.707W + 0.5X - 0.5Y`, peak-normalized
to -1 dBFS, sample-rate converted to 44.1 kHz, and encoded as stereo PCM16. Its
original two-second decay was preserved.
